# FNP-Sales-Analysis-Dashboard-Excel

## Project Overview

The FNP Sales Analysis Dashboard is an interactive Excel-based business intelligence solution designed to analyze sales performance across multiple dimensions, including revenue, customer spending, occasions, product categories, cities and order patterns. The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor key business metrics and identify trends that support data-driven decision-making.


## Business Objective

The primary objective of this project is to provide management with a centralized view of sales performance, helping them:

* Monitor overall sales performance
* Track revenue and order trends
* Analyze customer spending behavior
* Identify high-performing products and categories
* Evaluate sales performance across occasions and cities
* Support data-driven business decisions through interactive visualizations


## Tools & Technologies Used

* Microsoft Excel
* Power Query (Data Cleaning & Transformation)
* Pivot Tables
* Pivot Charts
* Slicers & Timelines
* Dashboard Design and Data Visualization
* Excel Formulas and Data Modeling

 

## Key Metrics

* **Total Revenue:** ₹35.2L
* **Total Orders:** 1,000
* **Average Customer Spend:** ₹3,520.98
* **Average Delivery Time:** 5.53 Days


## Dashboard Features

### Revenue Analysis

* Category-wise Revenue
* Occasion-wise Revenue
* Monthly Revenue Trend
* Revenue by Order Hour

### Product Analysis

* Top 5 Revenue-Generating Products
* Product Performance Comparison

### Customer Analysis

* Average Customer Spending
* Customer Purchase Trends

### Geographic Analysis

* Top 10 Cities by Order Volume

### Interactive Filtering

* Order Date Filter
* Delivery Date Filter
* Occasion Filter

## Dashboard Insights

* Colors generated the highest revenue among all product categories.
* Anniversary and Raksha Bandhan were among the highest revenue-generating occasions.
* Revenue showed seasonal peaks during specific months of the year.
* Magnam Set emerged as one of the top-performing products.
* Certain cities contributed significantly to total order volume.
* Order hour analysis revealed peak purchasing periods that can support operational planning.


## Business Impact

The dashboard helps stakeholders:

* Monitor sales performance in real time
* Identify key revenue drivers
* Understand customer purchasing behavior
* Optimize marketing and inventory strategies
* Make informed, data-driven business decisions

This dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of FNP sales performance, enabling business users to quickly identify revenue drivers, customer trends and operational opportunities.
